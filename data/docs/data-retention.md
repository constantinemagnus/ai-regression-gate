# Data retention

> Brightwell Cloud is a fictional company. These pages exist only to test an AI support bot.

## [data-1] Active workspaces

Workspace data is kept for as long as the subscription is active.

## [data-2] After cancellation

After a subscription is cancelled, the workspace stays available in read-only mode for 30 days so its data can be exported. After those 30 days, the data is deleted within a further 30 days, and backups are purged within 90 days of cancellation.

## [data-3] Data export

CSV and JSON exports are available on every plan. Business plans can also schedule automatic exports to an Amazon S3 bucket.

## [data-4] Data location

Workspace data is stored either in the EU (Frankfurt) or in the US (Virginia). The region is chosen when the workspace is created and cannot be changed later.

## [data-5] Audit logs

Audit logs are available only on the Business plan and are kept for 1 year.
