# Refunds

> Brightwell Cloud is a fictional company. These pages exist only to test an AI support bot.

## [refunds-1] Monthly plans

Monthly plans are not refunded for partial months. You can cancel at any time, and the workspace keeps full access until the end of the current billing period.

## [refunds-2] Annual plans

A new annual plan can be refunded in full if the refund is requested within 30 days of the first annual payment. Annual renewals are different: a renewal can be refunded only if the refund is requested within 7 days of the renewal date. After those windows, annual plans are not refunded, but you can switch to monthly billing at the next renewal.

## [refunds-3] How to request a refund

A billing admin requests a refund from Settings > Billing > Request refund. Approved refunds are paid back to the original payment method within 5–10 business days.

## [refunds-4] Add-ons

Usage-based add-ons, such as extra API calls and SMS credits, are not refunded once they have been consumed.

## [refunds-5] Outages

Downtime is compensated with service credits under the service level agreement, not with refunds.
