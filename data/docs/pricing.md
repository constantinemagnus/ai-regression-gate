# Pricing

> Brightwell Cloud is a fictional company. These pages exist only to test an AI support bot.

## [pricing-1] Plans and seats

Brightwell Cloud has three plans, billed per workspace. Starter costs $19 per month and allows up to 3 users. Team costs $49 per month and includes 10 users; each additional user on Team costs $6 per user per month. Business costs $199 per month and includes 25 users, single sign-on (SSO) and the audit log.

## [pricing-2] Annual billing

Paying annually gives two months free: you pay for 10 months and get 12. Annual prices are $190 per year for Starter, $490 per year for Team and $1,990 per year for Business.

## [pricing-3] Free trial

Every new workspace starts with a 14-day free trial of the Team plan. No credit card is needed to start the trial. If no plan is chosen by the end of the trial, the workspace becomes read-only until a plan is chosen.

## [pricing-4] Taxes

Prices do not include VAT or sales tax. Tax is added at checkout based on the billing address. EU business customers who provide a valid VAT ID are invoiced under the reverse-charge rule.

## [pricing-5] Changing plans

Upgrades take effect immediately, and the price difference for the rest of the billing period is charged on a prorated basis. Downgrades take effect at the next billing date; until then the workspace keeps the features of its current plan.

## [pricing-6] Nonprofit and education discount

Registered nonprofits and accredited schools get 40% off the Team and Business plans when billed annually. The discount requires verification through the billing settings and does not apply to monthly billing.
