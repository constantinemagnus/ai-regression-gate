# Service level agreement

> Brightwell Cloud is a fictional company. These pages exist only to test an AI support bot.

## [sla-1] Uptime commitment

Business plans are covered by a 99.9% monthly uptime commitment and Team plans by a 99.5% monthly uptime commitment. Starter plans have no uptime commitment, are not covered by the service level agreement and are not eligible for service credits after an outage.

## [sla-2] Service credits

If monthly uptime on a Business plan falls below 99.9%, the customer receives a credit of 10% of that month's fee. Below 99.0% the credit is 25%, and below 95.0% it is 50%. If monthly uptime on a Team plan falls below 99.5%, the credit is 10% of that month's fee.

## [sla-3] Claiming credits

Credits must be claimed through a support ticket within 30 days after the end of the affected month. Credits are applied to the next invoice and are never paid out in cash.

## [sla-4] Exclusions

Scheduled maintenance announced at least 72 hours in advance does not count as downtime, up to 4 hours per month. Outages caused by the customer's own systems or by force majeure events are also excluded.

## [sla-5] Support response times

For priority-1 incidents, Business customers get a first response within 1 hour, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. Team customers get a first response to priority-1 incidents within 8 business hours. Starter customers get email support with a first response within 2 business days.
